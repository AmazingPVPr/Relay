Original Relay activity cues; generated without recordings or third-party audio.
Run generate.py to regenerate mono 48 kHz 16-bit PCM WAVs (no playback).
The player converts them to stereo and applies the saved sound-effects volume.

Version 3 separates cue families by rhythm, register and timbre:
microphone: low wood taps; deafen: deeper double knocks; own connection:
warm chimes; participant arrival/departure: higher bubbles; broadcasting:
glass arpeggios; watching: rounded two/three-note cues; message: one high bell.
Opposite actions rise/fall with different rhythms. Smooth fades and headroom
are retained. Every cue can be auditioned individually in sound settings.
